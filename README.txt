SHUTTER Photography Website
============================

Files:
- index.html   = Home page
- contact.html = Contact page
- style.css    = Shared design and responsive layout

How to use:
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome.
3. Edit the text in index.html and contact.html to customize your site.
4. In contact.html, replace hello@example.com and the sample phone number with your own contact details.
5. Images are loaded from Unsplash, and Google Fonts are loaded online, so internet is needed for those assets.

Contact form note:
The contact form uses mailto and opens the visitor's email app. A working form that sends messages directly from the website needs a backend or a form service.
